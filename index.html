Learn about PMOS

Understanding PMOS

PMOS means Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome — the updated name for what many people still know as PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome).

Polyendocrine
More than one hormone system can be involved — not only the ovaries.
Metabolic
Weight, insulin, and long-term metabolic health are often part of the picture.
Ovarian
Cycles, ovulation, and fertility can be affected — without requiring "cyst disease."

Why the name changed from PCOS

"Polycystic" made many people think the condition was mainly about dangerous ovarian cysts. Research shows it is a broader hormone and metabolic syndrome — and the old name added stigma and delayed recognition.

How it became PMOS

A global consensus process chose Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome (PMOS). Full guideline alignment is targeted around 2028; older materials may still say PCOS.

Full rename story, sources, and in-depth screening literacy: Understanding PMOS on About · Early awareness · How P-Code works.
