About PMOS: Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome (PMOS), formerly known as PCOS, is a common hormone and metabolic condition. It may involve irregular cycles, skin/hair changes, weight or insulin concerns, mood changes, and fertility questions for some people.

Early awareness can support timely lifestyle steps and clinical follow-up. Why it is called PMOS now — research sources
